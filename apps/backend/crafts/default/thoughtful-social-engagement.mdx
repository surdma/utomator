# Thoughtful social engagement

## Step: scope | Choose contribution goal
<a id="step-scope" />
<CraftNode kind="brief" x={80} y={130} />

Identify an authentic professional community, discussion topic and useful contribution purpose.

**Next:** [evidence](#step-evidence)

## Step: evidence | Study accessible conversation
<a id="step-evidence" />
<CraftNode kind="research" x={390} y={130} />

Review owner-supplied or permitted discussion context, existing replies and claims that need checking.

**Next:** [develop](#step-develop)

## Step: develop | Draft useful context-aware reply
<a id="step-develop" />
<CraftNode kind="draft" x={700} y={130} />

Offer a concise informed answer, relevant evidence or genuine question, not vague flattery or engagement bait.

**Next:** [approval](#step-approval)

## Step: approval | Review owner-operated action
<a id="step-approval" />
<CraftNode kind="review" x={1010} y={130} />

Ask the owner to review and post manually unless a separately authorized provider action actually exists.

**Next:** [outcome](#step-outcome)

## Step: outcome | Track valuable dialogue
<a id="step-outcome" />
<CraftNode kind="measure" x={1320} y={130} />

Record confirmed replies, new substantive connections and respectful follow-up decisions.

**Next:** End
