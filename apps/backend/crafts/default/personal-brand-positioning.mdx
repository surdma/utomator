# Personal brand positioning

## Step: goal | Define the objective
<a id="step-goal" />
<CraftNode kind="brief" x={70} y={140} />

Identify the audience, requested outcome, platform, constraints and source of truth.

**Next:** [evidence](#step-evidence)

## Step: evidence | Gather verified evidence
<a id="step-evidence" />
<CraftNode kind="research" x={390} y={140} />

Review portfolio facts, current page or job description. Mark missing facts as unknown, never invent them.

**Next:** [craft](#step-craft)

## Step: craft | Craft the deliverable
<a id="step-craft" />
<CraftNode kind="draft" x={710} y={140} />

Draft concise, specific writing that fits the audience and platform. Preserve source attribution.

**Next:** [review](#step-review)

## Step: review | Review and refine
<a id="step-review" />
<CraftNode kind="review" x={1030} y={140} />

Check accuracy, originality, tone, platform rules, and whether the owner approves any publishing.

**Next:** End
