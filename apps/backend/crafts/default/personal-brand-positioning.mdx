# Personal brand positioning

## Step: scope | Identify career positioning
<a id="step-scope" />
<CraftNode kind="brief" x={80} y={130} />

Specify target roles, niche, ideal professional audience, and the opportunity this positioning should create.

**Next:** [evidence](#step-evidence)

## Step: evidence | Collect proof of expertise
<a id="step-evidence" />
<CraftNode kind="research" x={390} y={130} />

Collect dated responsibilities, recognized achievements, projects, work samples and approved voice examples. Separate evidence from guesses.

**Next:** [develop](#step-develop)

## Step: develop | Choose a distinct message
<a id="step-develop" />
<CraftNode kind="draft" x={700} y={130} />

Compare evidence-based positioning statements, role-specific headline and About candidates grounded in actual experience.

**Next:** [approval](#step-approval)

## Step: approval | Align public profile assets
<a id="step-approval" />
<CraftNode kind="review" x={1010} y={130} />

Align headline, About, Featured links and portfolio claims. Request owner approval for manual profile changes.

**Next:** [outcome](#step-outcome)

## Step: outcome | Check credibility and fit
<a id="step-outcome" />
<CraftNode kind="measure" x={1320} y={130} />

Verify every claim, consistency across surfaces and relevant future recruiter or collaborator outcomes.

**Next:** End
