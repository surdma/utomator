# Content series planning

## Step: scope | Define the series outcome
<a id="step-scope" />
<CraftNode kind="brief" x={80} y={130} />

Choose target professionals, a precise editorial question and a plausible opportunity outcome.

**Next:** [evidence](#step-evidence)

## Step: evidence | Research original material
<a id="step-evidence" />
<CraftNode kind="research" x={390} y={130} />

Gather original work, relevant project lessons, licensed sources and actual audience questions.

**Next:** [develop](#step-develop)

## Step: develop | Plan related episodes
<a id="step-develop" />
<CraftNode kind="draft" x={700} y={130} />

Build a sequence of distinct posts with specific hooks, examples, evidence, accessible media and calls to action.

**Next:** [approval](#step-approval)

## Step: approval | Review publishing permissions
<a id="step-approval" />
<CraftNode kind="review" x={1010} y={130} />

Check factual accuracy, originality, voice, citations, platform restrictions and owner approval before any publication.

**Next:** [outcome](#step-outcome)

## Step: outcome | Measure worthwhile response
<a id="step-outcome" />
<CraftNode kind="measure" x={1320} y={130} />

Track genuine discussions, useful contacts, portfolio visits or meeting requests when sources support them.

**Next:** End
