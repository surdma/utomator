# Job search research

## Step: scope | Specify career criteria
<a id="step-scope" />
<CraftNode kind="brief" x={80} y={130} />

Set target roles, locations, work authorization, experience level and priority conditions.

**Next:** [evidence](#step-evidence)

## Step: evidence | Verify fresh vacancies
<a id="step-evidence" />
<CraftNode kind="research" x={390} y={130} />

Collect authentic vacancy URLs, employer identity, requirements and posted dates. Treat inaccessible or stale openings as unverified.

**Next:** [develop](#step-develop)

## Step: develop | Assess role fit
<a id="step-develop" />
<CraftNode kind="draft" x={700} y={130} />

Compare essential qualifications against documented experience and call out gaps or missing evidence.

**Next:** [approval](#step-approval)

## Step: approval | Prioritize opportunities
<a id="step-approval" />
<CraftNode kind="review" x={1010} y={130} />

Rank openings by fit, role preference and opportunity relevance without claiming an offer or interview.

**Next:** [outcome](#step-outcome)

## Step: outcome | Prepare the research handoff
<a id="step-outcome" />
<CraftNode kind="measure" x={1320} y={130} />

Provide a sourced shortlist, needed resume evidence and owner-controlled application next steps.

**Next:** End
