# About page: content ownership and attribution (October 2026)

About is a professional profile, not another project gallery. Its sections are:
introduction, biography and portrait, three professional identities,
technical skills, experience, and education.

Project examples, case studies, open-source tools and path-planning demonstrations
are maintained under /projects. Do not reintroduce Selected Evidence, Outside
the Product Roadmap, the duplicate capability explorer or the process cards on About.

## MilliHub experience and attribution

The portfolio lists MilliHub as a current role without an inferred start year.
A repository timestamp is not an employment record. Dates and role scope
should be published only when confirmed directly by the portfolio owner.
No contribution timestamps should appear in the career narrative.

The CAE / VP Engineering role is supplied by the portfolio owner. AEKO and
SabiGo project contributions are described in their individual case studies.
Avoid implying sole ownership of the products, the underlying protocol, or
unverified revenue, adoption, headcount or performance improvements.
